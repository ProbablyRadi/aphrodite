# Rens Adisea — historical 2022 backstory

Imported 26 October 2026. The six events, all 246 classification rows, cars,
entrants, flags and championship points come from the linked historical pages.
Wikipedia factual tables were parsed with row/column spans preserved; chassis
and engine subrows were deduplicated by car number. Source time/gap strings are
preserved. Retirement reasons are separate from missing gaps and appear as DNF
tooltips. NC and DNS remain distinct from DNF.

## Fictional adaptation

Only Rens has a 2022 season. #56 Team Project 1 is crewed by Rens Adisea
(Netherlands), Ben Barnicoat and Brendan Iribe in every race. This deliberately
replaces the historical crew changes. All #56 car results stay historical:
GTE Am finishes 3rd, DNF, DNF, 10th, 8th, 2nd; 55 points; seventh in the GTE Am
team standings. His career overview uses this entrant position, rather than an
invented official driver-championship position. He has six starts, no wins,
two podiums and no class poles.

GTE Pro and GTE Am stay separate to preserve class scoring; there was no GT3
class in the 2022 championship. LMP2 includes Pro/Am entrants in the main LMP2
classification; its separate Pro/Am trophy is not duplicated as another class.
Hypercar/GTE Pro entrant totals follow the relevant full-season driver's
published points. LMP2/GTE Am use published team standings. These entrant views
are not the separate historical manufacturer championships.

Scoring uses the published six-hour, eight-hour and 24-hour scales, with class
pole bonuses. All 39 eligible entrant championship totals match the source tables.
Explicit per-race awards account for pole points and invitational eligibility;
invitational Le Mans cars retain their actual positions but score no WEC points.
Non-participation in a completed historical round adds no points, while missing
results in the fictional game seasons retain their existing unknown handling.
