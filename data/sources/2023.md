# Custom 2023 career setup

The user supplied the seven-round calendar and all 38 entrant rows, including
class order, car numbers, team names, zero points and blank results.
This setup is used independently by Rens and Jordan; it does not import real
2023 race results or alter either character's personal car/team history.

- 13 Hypercars, 14 LMGTE entries and 11 LMP2 entries.
- Daytona, Portimão, Spa, Le Mans, Monza, Fuji and Road Atlanta.
- Round headers: DAY, POR, BEL, FRA, ITA, JPN and ATL.
- Source order is preserved while points and completed results remain tied.
- #57 Kessel Racing's missing hash prefix in the supplied table is normalised.

Team nationality flags and manufacturer/model identities were checked against
the [2023 WEC entry list](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_FIA_World_Endurance_Championship#Entries).
The custom calendar, names and roster remain the user's version.
